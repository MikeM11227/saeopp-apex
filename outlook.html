Salary timing and whether October premiums have cleared require confirmation. Remaining payroll taxes or transition costs were not provided.

Hotel cash commitments: Omni $41,675.00 in November or February; Ballantyne $8,000.00 on January 29; Westin $5,000.00 on February 1. Omni is counted once. Direct billing changes timing, not the six-month total, and the complete conference master bill remains to be budgeted.

What is missing from the model

No new conference registrations, membership, sponsorship, training or grant receipts are assumed. Sphere incremental receipts/costs, conference settlement beyond the disclosed deposit, later card cycles, other operating expenses, refunds, merchant fees, remaining Ballantyne obligations and spring scholarship disbursements are excluded because amounts or dates were not provided. These omissions can move results in either direction.

The brief’s mid-November cash projection above $180,000.00 cannot be reproduced from the supplied dated flows. Rebuild it from weekly receipts and payments before relying on it. Month-end balances can hide earlier intra-month shortfalls.

RECOMMENDATIONS / COLLECTIONS, CONTRIBUTION & RETENTION

Build a repeatable growth engine.

RECOMMENDATION / 01

Collect existing demand first

Assign an invoice owner, procurement contact and promised payment date to every valid institutional order. Prioritize Masterclass $29,430.00 base and F4F $8,000.00; then conference $38,420.00, APR $3,580.00 and pre-conference $2,394.00. Measure weekly collected dollars, invoice age and missed promises. These are existing receivables, not new sales.

RECOMMENDATION / 02

Grow by contribution, not registrations

Demand-test remote training and institutional bundles at existing price points. Require paid-enrollment thresholds and cost caps. Masterclass base value is $63,270.00 against $63,457.48 stated direct cost: a $187.48 shortfall before Sphere effects. Sphere-inclusive gross less that cost is $7,012.52 before additional Sphere costs and overhead. Establish the full cost of F4F delivery before calling its revenue pure margin.

RECOMMENDATION / 03

Make membership a retention engine

Separate individual and institutional ledger categories and resolve the unitemized $5,232.00. Build renewal cohorts, lapse outreach, a benefits calendar and program-level IMP invoicing. Measure paid renewals and retention against the eligible renewal cohort. Do not annualize October’s first 15 days without seasonal history.

RECOMMENDATION / 04

Match contracts to cash

Seek written direct billing, staged deposits and payment dates aligned with collections. Maintain a calendar of deposits, attrition, cancellation terms and minimum spending. Require an event budget and a 13-week cash test before signing a new venue commitment. Deferral changes timing; it does not eliminate liability.

RECOMMENDATION / 05

Adopt a funded reserve policy

Use $6,790.47, equal to three months of Kaiser and Principal premiums, as a provisional benefits-only planning floor. It is not three months of total operating costs. Calculate full unrestricted recurring expenses and have the board set the reserve target, permitted uses, approval rules and replenishment schedule. Fund it from realized unrestricted surplus after obligations.

RECOMMENDATION / 06

Replace G5 support structurally

Fund the continuing $2,263.49 monthly benefits from recurring unrestricted contribution after the final salary payment. Separate event pass-through cash from available contribution. Reforecast the near-break-even annual budget with an explicit reserve contribution, then use post-event contribution statements to decide what to repeat or expand.
